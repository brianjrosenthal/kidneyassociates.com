Welcome to Kidney Associates

 

Kidney Associates, PLLC is a Houston-based nephrology practice founded in 1980 and dedicated to comprehensive kidney care. We provide services in chronic kidney disease management, dialysis, transplantation, and consultative nephrology, serving patients throughout Houston and the surrounding communities. We’re committed to excellence, integrity, and patient-centered care.
